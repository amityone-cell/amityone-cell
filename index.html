A photographic journal — 2021–2026

Quiet
Distances

Six places. Six mornings I couldn't put the camera down. Scroll to walk through them in order.

01

The Lake Before Anyone Wakes

Muskoka, Ontario — 5:42 AM — 24mm, f/8

Three mornings in a row I rowed out before the fog lifted, waiting for the one minute the water goes completely still.

02

Alleyway, No Name

Chefchaouen, Morocco — 8:15 PM — 35mm, f/2

I never found this street on a map again. Some places you only get to visit once, and that's fine.

03

One Tree, Every Season

Cotswolds, England — 6:50 AM — 50mm, f/5.6

I've photographed this same tree four times now. This is the frame I keep coming back to.

04

Low Tide

Essaouira, Morocco — 7:30 AM — 24mm, f/11

The boats look stranded for about twenty minutes a day. I planned an entire trip around that window.

05

Minus Twelve

Nagano, Japan — 7:05 AM — 85mm, f/4

My hands stopped working properly after ten minutes. Worth it for the way the steam held its shape.

06

Walking Toward Nothing in Particular

Salar de Uyuni, Bolivia — 4:20 PM — 70mm, f/9

That's me in the frame, actually — my tripod, a ten-second timer, and about four hundred attempts.
